# 📜 Rules

1. **Be kind.** No bullying, insults, hate or harassment.
2. **Keep personal info private.** Never share your real name, address, school, phone number, passwords or social media – not yours, not anyone else's.
3. **No scams.** No "free Robux", fake links, account trading or selling.
4. **No trading for Robux or real money.**
5. **Keep it family friendly.** No NSFW, gore or shocking content.
6. **No spam.** No message floods, mass pings or invite links to other servers.
7. **Use the right channel.** Bugs → #bug-reports, ideas → #suggestions, help → #support.
8. **Listen to the moderators.** Problems? Contact a Moderator in #support – staff will never DM you first and will never ask for your password.

**What happens if rules are broken:** reminder → timeout → kick → ban (serious cases skip steps).
You must be old enough to use Discord in your country (13+ in most countries).
