# ❓ FAQ

**How do I get feathers faster?**
Buy new pillows in the Shop, upgrade Feather Power, unlock new zones – and hit the training dummies for a bonus.

**What is Rebirth?**
You reset feathers, upgrades and zones but keep your pillows and get a permanent feather multiplier.

**Where do I enter codes?**
Settings (⚙️) → Code → REDEEM. New codes are posted in #codes.

**Is there a bonus for playing with friends?**
Yes! +10 % feathers per friend in your server (up to +30 %).

**I found a bug!**
Post it in #bug-reports or use the 💬 FEEDBACK button in the game settings.

**Did I lose my progress?**
Progress saves automatically. If something looks wrong, ask in #support – never share your password.
