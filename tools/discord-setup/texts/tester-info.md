# 🧪 Tester info

Thanks for testing! You play an early version – bugs are expected, and finding them helps a lot.

**How to test (20–30 min per session, ideally on 2–3 different days)**
1. Play the tutorial without skipping it.
2. Play normally: buy pillows, upgrades, unlock zones, try a rebirth.
3. Try: Daily reward, settings, playing with a friend, playing on a phone.
4. Report with the 💬 FEEDBACK button (Settings) or in #tester-feedback: *what happened, where, what you expected*.
